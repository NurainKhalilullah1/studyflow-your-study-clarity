# StudyFlow 16.0.0

_Released September 18, 2026_

StudyFlow 16.0.0 introduces an all new analytics dashboard to track your study habits and mastery, rich ambient soundscapes in the Focus Room, and spaced repetition flashcards designed to help you prepare for exams with confidence.

## Highlights

- **Analytics Dashboard**: View your study streak, total focus hours, and subject mastery all in one screen with actionable recommendations on what to revise next.
- **Ambient Focus Room**: Listen to calming soundscapes like rain, ocean waves, coffee shop chatter, and binaural beats to keep you immersed during deep study sessions.
- **Spaced Repetition Flashcards**: Review cards using a scientifically proven schedule that adapts to how easily you remember each concept.
- **AI Voice Tutor**: Practice speaking and discussing difficult topics naturally with your AI study partner.

## Improvements

- **Instant Mobile Access**: When opening the Android application, you are taken straight to your dashboard or login without having to pass through the landing page.
- **Email Verification Codes**: Enjoy stronger account protection with one time verification codes delivered directly to your inbox upon sign in.
- **Flexible Analytics Filters**: Switch between 7 days, 30 days, and all time views to inspect both your recent momentum and full semester achievements.
- **Smart Study Suggestions**: Receive instant links to practice quizzes or flashcards whenever your mastery drops below target levels in any subject.

## Fixes

- Fixed a problem where quiz question generation could stall on large course documents.
- Fixed an issue where long inactive sessions remained open on shared devices by introducing a rolling seven day inactivity check.
- Fixed layout quirks in the focus room audio player on small mobile screens.

## Upgrade notes

If you use the Android application, download and install the new version 16.0.0 update package. Web users receive all updates automatically upon refreshing the page.
